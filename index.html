📝 Step-by-Step

Step 1 — /h/

Start with your mouth in a relaxed position. Breathe out gently — you should feel a soft stream of air passing through your throat.

Step 2 — /æ/ (the main vowel)

✅ Open your mouth wide — lower your jaw naturally
✅ Keep your tongue forward and low in your mouth
✅ Lips are relaxed — not rounded, not spread too wide
✅ The tip of your tongue gently touches your lower teeth
💬 Say: "aaa" — short and crisp, not too long

Step 3 — /t/

Raise the tip of your tongue and press it firmly against the ridge just behind your upper teeth. Release it sharply — a small puff of air comes out. Your voice stops completely.

Putting it together

/h/ + /æ/ + /t/ = "hat"
Try it again — slowly at first, then a little faster:
haaat → hat

🔊 Vowel Information

  • Symbol: [æ] — Near-open front unrounded vowel
  • Height: Open (low) — tongue is low in the mouth
  • Backness: Front — tongue is forward in the mouth
  • Lips: Unrounded — lips are relaxed
